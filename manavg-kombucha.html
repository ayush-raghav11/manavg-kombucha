Our Home

Brewed where the Ganga leaves the mountains

Rishikesh sits where the Ganga leaves the Himalayan foothills and enters the plains. Clean air, cool mornings and a deep tradition of mindful living shape how we brew: unhurried, careful and honest.

Our brewery in Rishikesh combines that spirit with modern food-safety practice, so tradition and quality go in together.

Why Rishikesh

  • Cool, clean foothill climate
    Steady conditions suit slow, controlled fermentation.
  • Pure water, treated with care
    Multi-stage filtration and testing before every brew, inspired by the clarity of the Ganga.
  • Close to Himalayan herbs
    Short supply routes from hill growers to our brew house.
  • A culture of mindful living
    The yoga capital's calm shapes our unhurried craft.

Our Process

From mountain herb to sealed bottle

Consistency comes from a controlled, documented process, not luck.

Filter & Brew

Multi-stage filtered water, premium tea and measured sugar, steeped at controlled temperature.

Ferment

A healthy SCOBY ferments in food-grade vessels, with pH and temperature logged daily.

Infuse Herbs

Himalayan herbs and fruit go into a second fermentation for flavour and natural fizz.

Test, Bottle, Seal

Each batch is checked before filling sanitised glass bottles and sealing.
